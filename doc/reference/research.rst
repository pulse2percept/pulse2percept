=====================
Selected Publications
=====================

pulse2percept has been used in studies of retinal and cortical prosthetic
vision, phosphene simulation, stimulus optimization, perceptual learning, and
computer vision for visual prostheses.

A few examples from research groups outside the core pulse2percept development
team:

* Y Wu, DDT Nguyen, H Konermann, R Yilmaz, P Walter, J Stegmaier (2025).
  *Visual Fixation-Based Retinal Prosthetic Simulation.*
  **2025 IEEE 22nd International Symposium on Biomedical Imaging (ISBI)**.
  doi:`10.1109/ISBI60581.2025.10980954 
  <https://doi.org/10.1109/ISBI60581.2025.10980954>`_

* T Fauvel & M Chalk (2021).
  *Human-in-the-loop optimization of visual prosthetic stimulation.*
  **Journal of Neural Engineering** 19(3).
  doi:`10.1088/1741-2552/ac7615
  <https://doi.org/10.1088/1741-2552/ac7615>`_

* A Lozano et al. (2020).
  *Neurolight: A Deep Learning Neural Interface for Cortical Visual
  Prostheses.*
  **International Journal of Neural Systems**.
  doi:`10.1142/S0129065720500458
  <https://doi.org/10.1142/S0129065720500458>`_

* J Steffen, G Hille, and K Tönnies (2019).
  *Automatic Perception Enhancement for Simulated Retinal Implants.*
  **Proceedings of ICPRAM 2019**.
  doi:`10.5220/0007695409080914
  <https://doi.org/10.5220/0007695409080914>`_

This list is intentionally incomplete. For a current list of publications
